Image Rotator

Rotate or flip an image in your browser and download the result.

Click to choose a file or drag and drop it here

What is the Image Rotator?

This tool rotates an image in 90-degree steps, or flips it horizontally or vertically, so you can quickly fix a sideways photo or mirror an image, without opening a full photo editor.

How to use it

  1. Choose an image from your device.
  2. Click Rotate Left/Right to turn it 90° at a time, or Flip Horizontal/Vertical to mirror it — each click applies to the current result, so you can combine rotations and flips.
  3. Click Reset to start over from the original, or Download PNG when you're happy with the result.

A sideways photo is one of the most common small annoyances after taking a picture with a phone held at an angle, and many upload forms don't auto-correct orientation. Rotating by exact 90-degree steps keeps the image perfectly sharp, since it simply repositions pixels rather than resampling them the way an arbitrary-angle rotation would. This tool works entirely on your device using the Canvas API, so the image is never uploaded anywhere.
