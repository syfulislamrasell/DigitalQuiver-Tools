What is the Age Calculator?

Enter a date of birth and this tool works out the exact age as of today (or any date you choose) — broken down into full years, months and days, along with the total number of days and weeks lived, and a countdown to the next birthday.

How to use it

  1. Pick the date of birth using the date field.
  2. Leave "Calculate age as of" set to today, or change it to see the age on a specific past or future date.
  3. Click Calculate Age to see the full breakdown.

The calculation accounts for the actual number of days in each month and for leap years, so the years/months/days figure matches how people naturally describe age rather than relying on a rough 365-day average. This is useful for filling out forms that ask for exact age, checking eligibility rules with a minimum age requirement, planning an anniversary or birthday countdown, or simply satisfying curiosity about how many days you've been alive. The "as of" date option also makes it possible to calculate someone's age at a specific historical event or a future milestone date, not just today.
