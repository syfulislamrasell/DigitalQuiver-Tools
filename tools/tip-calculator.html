What is the Tip Calculator?

This tool calculates how much to tip on a bill, the total amount including the tip, and how much each person owes when the bill is split evenly among a group. Pick a common tip percentage with one tap, or enter any custom percentage you prefer.

How to use it

  1. Enter the bill amount before tip.
  2. Choose a preset tip percentage or type a custom one.
  3. Enter how many people are splitting the bill to see the per-person amount.

Tipping customs vary a lot from country to country and even between types of service — restaurants, delivery, salons and taxis often have different norms — so this calculator deliberately leaves the percentage entirely up to you rather than assuming one region's standard. Splitting the total evenly is the simplest approach for a group meal; if people ordered very different amounts, you may prefer to calculate each person's tip on their own portion instead, but the even split covers the vast majority of everyday cases quickly.
