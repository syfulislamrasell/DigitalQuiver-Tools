What is the KDP Royalty Calculator?

This tool estimates what you'll actually earn per sale on Kindle Direct Publishing, using Amazon's own published royalty formulas for both eBooks and paperbacks — not a rough percentage guess.

eBook royalty formula

The 70% royalty plan applies only to list prices between $2.99 and $12.99 (this ceiling was raised from $9.99 in July 2026); Amazon also deducts a delivery fee of $0.15 per megabyte of your file size before paying out the 70%. Outside that price range, or on the 35% plan, there's no delivery fee, but the base rate is lower. For many small, text-only eBooks the 70% plan wins; for large, image-heavy files the delivery fee can eat much of the advantage — this calculator shows you the actual number either way.

Paperback royalty formula

Paperback royalty = (List Price × 60% if priced at $9.99 or above, else 50%) minus the printing cost. Printing cost = $1.00 fixed fee plus a per-page cost that depends on interior type: roughly $0.012/page for black & white, $0.0255/page for standard color, and $0.065/page for premium color (US marketplace).

A note on accuracy

Amazon periodically updates its royalty rates, price thresholds and printing costs. This calculator uses Amazon's most recently published formulas as of 2026 (verified against current publishing guides); double-check against your KDP Bookshelf's own royalty estimate before finalizing your pricing, since Amazon's dashboard always reflects the live, current numbers.
