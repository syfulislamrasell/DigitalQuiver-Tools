ASIN Extractor

Extract ASINs from any Amazon product URL, one per line.

What is the ASIN Extractor?

An ASIN (Amazon Standard Identification Number) is the 10-character code Amazon assigns to every product listing. This tool pulls the ASIN out of any Amazon product URL — useful for building product feeds, checking competitor listings in bulk, or feeding ASINs into other seller tools that require the bare code rather than a full link.

How to use it

  1. Paste one or more Amazon product URLs, one per line — any format works, including short links, URLs with tracking parameters, or the classic /dp/ASIN and /gp/product/ASIN patterns.
  2. Click Extract ASINs (or just keep typing — it updates live).
  3. Copy the resulting list, one ASIN per line, with duplicates automatically removed.

Everything happens in your browser using pattern matching against the URL text — no data is sent anywhere, so it's safe to paste bulk lists of competitor or supplier URLs.
